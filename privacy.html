Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026.

These apps ("Verified Today", "Know Bit Daily", "AI Daily Decoded", "The Money Blueprint studio") are private tools run by the owner of the matching YouTube channels. They are not offered to the public and have no other users.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission, an app uses the YouTube Data API scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly to upload videos to the owner's own channel and to read that channel's own video list and basic statistics.

What is stored

Only the OAuth credentials needed to act on the owner's own channel, kept in private, access-controlled repository secrets. No data about any other person is collected, stored, sold or shared, and no data is used for advertising.

Revoking access

The owner can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: open an issue or contact the owner through github.com/srikanthputta-hub.

The apps' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
