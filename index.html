§A About

I work at the point where clinical practice and clinical data meet. Trained first as a dentist, I moved into clinical data management and health informatics to work on the systems that keep trial and patient data accurate, structured, and usable — from electronic data capture in clinical trials to CRM-driven patient transport coordination today.

That path runs from Medidata Rave and Oracle Clinical EDC in Hyderabad, through patient data administration at Mater Private Network, to my current role at the Irish Cancer Society, where I use Microsoft Dynamics 365 to coordinate patient transport. Underneath it all is the same interest: how well-designed data systems make care easier to deliver.
